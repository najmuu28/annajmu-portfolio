# Annajmu Tsaqieb: Portfolio Website

A static, framework-free portfolio. Open index.html in a browser, or upload the folder to any static host.

## Files
- index.html: page structure and content
- style.css: all styling. Colors and fonts are tokens in :root at the top
- script.js: mobile menu, active nav link, scroll fade-in, portrait fallback
- images/profile.jpg: your portrait (add this file, see below)

## Things to do
1. Add your portrait at images/profile.jpg (about 680 x 880 px, JPG). Until then the page shows an A.T monogram.
2. "Open to projects" in the hero is a status you control. Delete the <p class="floating-note"> line in index.html if it is not true.
3. Project cards are practice areas with "Case study coming soon". When a real project is ready, turn the card into a link to its case study and replace the status text.
4. Add a favicon of your own when you have a logo (the page currently uses an empty one).

## Editing
- Email and social links: the Contact section of index.html
- Colors: --purple, --purple-bright and --bg in style.css
- Keep text contrast at 4.5:1 or higher if you change colors
